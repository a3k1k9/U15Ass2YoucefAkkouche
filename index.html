Cat Care & House Rules

A short read before your visit so everyone — cats included — has a good time.

Before you visit

  • Please arrive on time — sessions are kept short so the cats get proper rest between visits.
  • Let us know about any cat allergies when booking.
  • Children under 12 must be with an adult at all times.
  • No outside food or drink for the cats, please — it can make them ill.

While you're here

  • Let the cat come to you rather than chasing or cornering them.
  • Some cats don't like being picked up — check with staff first.
  • Please don't wake a sleeping cat.
  • Use the hand sanitiser provided before and after handling the cats.
  • No flash photography.

How we care for the cats

Health checks

All cats are vaccinated, microchipped and neutered. A vet visits monthly, and any cat that seems unwell is moved to a quiet room away from guests.

Rest time

The café closes for an hour at lunchtime and every Monday so the cats get a proper break from visitors.

Safe spaces

Every cat has access to a raised shelf or hideaway they can retreat to whenever they've had enough company.
